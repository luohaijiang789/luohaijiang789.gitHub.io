## 先分清地点与动作

station 是车站，platform 是站台，stop 可指站点；ticket 是车票，fare 是票价。leave / depart 表出发，arrive 表到达，change / transfer 在交通语境可涉及换乘。

**How do I get to the station?** 问路线。**Which platform does the train leave from?** 问站台。**Does this bus go to the city centre?** 确认是否到达目标地。

## 一段出行对话

**You:** Excuse me, does this train go to Riverside?

**Staff:** Yes, but you need to change at Central.

**You:** So I change at Central. Which platform should I use there?

**Staff:** Check the departure board when you arrive.

**You:** Thank you. How long does the journey usually take?

对方要求到站查看信息，就不要把这次对话当作永远有效的站台保证。交通安排会变化，现场信息需再次核对。

## 听方向时抓三个部分

动作：go straight / turn left / cross the road。

参照物：at the traffic lights / opposite the bank / next to the library。

终点：on your right / at the end of the street。

复述：“So I go straight, turn left at the lights, and the station is on my right. Is that correct?” 比只说 OK 更容易发现误解。

## 延误与改动

**The train has been delayed.** 注意 have been + 过去分词。

**Is there an alternative route?** 问替代方案。**Will I need a different ticket?** 确认票务条件，不自行推断原票是否通用。

## 练习

1. 询问去机场通常需要多久。
2. 确认是否需要换车。
3. 对方给出三步路线后，用自己的话复述。

<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

1. How long does it usually take to get to the airport?
2. Do I need to change trains? 或 Is this a direct service? 两者询问方式稍不同。
3. 保留动作、参照点和终点，听不清时指出具体部分：Where do I turn left?
