## 先找句中有没有可用的助动词

| 原句 | 问句 | 否定 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| She is ready. | Is she ready? | She isn't ready. |
| They can help. | Can they help? | They can't help. |
| You have finished. | Have you finished? | You haven't finished. |
| She works here. | Does she work here? | She doesn't work here. |
| They arrived. | Did they arrive? | They didn't arrive. |

实义动词的一般现在/过去常借 do/does/did；后面的实义动词回到原形。be、情态和完成体助动词各按自身结构处理。

## 主语提问与宾语提问不同

**Who called you?** who 本身是主语，通常不再加 did 进行普通主语提问。

**Who did you call?** you 是主语，who 是被打电话的对象，因此出现 did。

## 间接疑问使用陈述语序

直接：Where is the station?

间接：Could you tell me **where the station is**? 不是 where is the station。类似 I don't know why it failed，不写成 why did it fail。

一般疑问嵌入时可用 whether / if：Do you know whether the room is available? 这里 if 不是条件“如果”。

## 附加问句与否定问句

You're available, aren't you? 常用于核对，但语气与语调会影响含义。对 “Don't you agree?” 的 yes/no 回答应围绕事实理解：Yes, I do 表示同意；No, I don't 表示不同意。容易混淆时直接重复完整意思。

## 练习

1. 改错：Where you work?
2. 把 “When does the meeting start?” 放进 Could you tell me…。
3. 区别 Who helped you? 与 Who did you help?

<details><summary>答案与解释</summary>

1. Where do you work?
2. Could you tell me when the meeting starts?
3. 前者问谁帮助你；后者问你帮助了谁。主语与对象不同。

</details>
