## 转述首先是改变参照点

Maya 说：**“I am busy today.”** 你后来转述：**Maya said that she was busy that day.** 人称、时间表达和动词形式都可能改变，因为说话的人与时间变了。

| 原话 | 常见转述变化 |
| --- | --- |
| I am ready. | She said she was ready. |
| I have finished. | She said she had finished. |
| I will call you. | She said she would call me. |
| Can you help? | She asked whether I could help. |
| Where is the file? | She asked where the file was. |

变化不能脱离事实。若内容仍成立，或者说话者要强调当前真实，时态未必必须后移。today 是否改成 that day，也取决于转述是否仍发生在同一天。

## say、tell、ask 的结构

**say something / say that…**；若引入接收者，常用 say to someone。

**tell someone something / tell someone that…**，通常要有接收者：She told me that…，不写 She told that… 来替代一般用法。

**ask someone to do** 转述请求；**tell someone not to do** 转述否定指令：He told me not to share the file。

## 转述问题不保留直接问句语序

“Why did it fail?” → She asked **why it had failed**（按该时间参照）。重点是从句内使用陈述语序，不写 why had it failed。

转述还要区分事实与对方声称的内容：She said the test passed 不等于你已亲自验证。技术记录中保留消息来源。

## 练习

1. 改错：She said me that she was ready.
2. 转述请求 “Please review this file.”。
3. 对方昨天说 “I will call tomorrow”，你今天怎样转述时间？

<details><summary>答案与解释</summary>

1. She told me that she was ready. 或 She said to me that she was ready.
2. She asked me to review the file.
3. 可能说 She said yesterday that she would call today. 使用实际日期关系，不机械替换词。

</details>
